---
title: "Understanding from First Principles"
date: "2026-09-18"
---

Most of what we call knowledge is accumulated convention and reasoning by analogy. We do things because someone before us did them, or because everyone around us agrees that is how they are done.

Reasoning from first principles requires stripping an idea of its inherited assumptions until only the irreducible truths remain. It is slower, uncomfortable, and often lonely, but it is the only reliable way to find unexpected connections between domains that seem completely separate on the surface.
